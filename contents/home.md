I am a B.S. candidate in **Data Science and Big Data Technology** at **Anhui University**. My work sits at the intersection of machine learning, data systems, and applied decision-making, with recent projects in biomedical AI, autonomous-driving data loops, embedded AI deployment, and quantitative research.

My current research interests include **graph learning**, **multi-modal representation learning**, **LLM and agentic systems**, **scientific machine learning**, and **data-centric model deployment**. I am especially interested in building models that connect heterogeneous data sources, support interpretable prediction, and remain usable in real engineering environments.

Beyond research, I have worked on industry-facing systems across asset management, ADAS data infrastructure, embedded AI, cloud architecture, consumer analytics, inference optimization, and computer vision. These experiences shaped my preference for models that are not only accurate in experiments, but also robust under messy data, constrained hardware, and changing operational requirements.

I am open to research collaboration, graduate research opportunities, and engineering work related to AI systems, computational biology, autonomous driving, and quantitative modeling.
