I am a Ph.D. student and Research Assistant in **Computer Engineering at Clemson University**, working with **Prof. Lan Zhang** on wireless AI systems, distributed learning, secure federated learning, and intelligent edge networks. I received my B.S. in **Data Science and Big Data Technology from Anhui University** in 2026, graduating with a 3.53 GPA and a top-5% rank.

My research interests span **wireless AI**, **secure federated and distributed learning**, **LLM post-training and verifier routing**, **graph neural networks**, **generative recommender systems**, **multi-agent systems**, and **scientific machine learning**. I am especially interested in learning systems that operate under realistic constraints: heterogeneous data, limited communication, privacy and security requirements, edge deployment, and decision-making feedback loops.

My current and recent work includes risk-controlled routing of verifier feedback for reliable policy improvement, role-aware prediction and ranking for lipid nanoparticle formulations, support-aware abnormal-gait screening, economic world models, multi-omics graph learning for triple-negative breast cancer, and agricultural knowledge extraction. Earlier research appointments at East China Normal University and Shanghai Jiao Tong University covered generative recommendation, multi-agent collaboration, graph learning, and mathematical optimization for the Cardinal Optimizer.

Beyond research, I have worked across AI commercialization strategy, quantitative investment research, ADAS data closed loops, embedded AI deployment, cloud and edge data architecture, and technology entrepreneurship. These experiences shape my broader goal: to develop intelligent systems that are technically rigorous, deployable, and useful in high-impact engineering, scientific, and economic settings.
