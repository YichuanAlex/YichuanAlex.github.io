My recent research experience includes generative recommender systems and multi-agent collaboration at East China Normal University, mathematical optimization for the Cardinal Optimizer at Shanghai Jiao Tong University, multi-omics graph learning for triple-negative breast cancer, LNP formulation property prediction, Parkinson's gait recognition, and knowledge extraction for agricultural decision support.

Beyond research, I have worked across AI commercialization strategy, quantitative investment research, ADAS data closed loops, embedded AI deployment, cloud and edge data architecture, and technology entrepreneurship. These experiences shape my broader goal: to develop intelligent systems that are technically rigorous, deployable, and useful in high-impact engineering and scientific settings.
